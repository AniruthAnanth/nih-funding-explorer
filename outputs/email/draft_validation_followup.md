**One correction to my last email.** I said department-level revenue is not
publicly available. That was too strong: there is no source that collects it

included. Our slide puts total research expenditures at **$74M, of which NIH is
$35M — 47%**. Worth keeping in view, because it means any NIH-based ranking,
including mine, sees about half of what the department actually spends and none
of the DOD, foundation, industry or philanthropic support.
